# Video Downloader Visual Direction

## Locked Direction (Item 1)

**Quiet Utility**: A minimalist, Apple HIG-aligned interface that makes asynchronous video downloading feel calm, transparent, and trustworthy.

## Experience Statement

- One clear primary action per screen.
- Status over decoration.
- High legibility, low cognitive load.
- Motion only where it clarifies system state.
- Progressive disclosure for advanced controls.

## Product Tone

- Clear
- Reassuring
- Precise
- Quietly premium

## UX Intent by Flow

1. Submit URL in one interaction.
2. Understand progress at a glance.
3. Download with confidence before expiry.
4. Recover quickly from errors with direct next actions.

## Apple HIG Interpretation for This Product

- Prioritize content hierarchy with generous spacing and restrained color.
- Use familiar control patterns and expected placements.
- Keep touch targets large and interactions predictable.
- Respect accessibility preferences (Dynamic Type, reduced motion, contrast).
- Avoid visual noise and unnecessary persistent chrome.

## Non-Goals

- Gamified UI patterns.
- Overly dense dashboards in primary user surfaces.
- Multiple competing CTAs in a single viewport.
